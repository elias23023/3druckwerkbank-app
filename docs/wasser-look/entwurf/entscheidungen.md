# Decisions of the owner (kept by the main chat; newest last)

These override older notes wherever they disagree.

1. **Buttons on a card show the card's water**, not the landscape behind the card. Buttons free over the scene bend the landscape.
2. **Colour: no blue.** Two tones were built: "dunkel" (neutral dark water, default) and "klar" (clear water), switched by `data-wa-ton="klar"` on `<html>`.
3. **Red of "Löschen":** strength B (deeper, quieter) on "dunkel", strength A (clear red) on "klar".
4. **Both tones stay, as the two modes of the app (13.11., 12:40).** He first wanted exactly one design without any switching; he changed his mind: "ich möchte jetzt doch hell und dunkel Modus wieder wie's geplant war ... beide Varianten von dir aktuell passen dafür gut".
   - Dark mode = tone "dunkel". Light mode = tone "klar".
   - The app already has the setting "Erscheinungsbild: Automatisch (Tageszeit) / Hell / Dunkel" (`settings.theme`; `src/App.tsx` sets `document.documentElement.dataset.theme`, and today forces "dunkel" on mobile). When the template is applied to the app, that setting chooses the tone on mobile AND desktop: Dunkel -> "dunkel", Hell -> "klar", Automatisch -> by the look of the hour (`DARK_LOOKS` in `src/scene/looks.ts`).
   - So neither tone will be deleted. A tone is still only a block of token values.
   - The app's old light theme (light panels with dark text, `:root[data-theme="hell"]` in `src/styles/tokens.css`) is NOT what he means: the light mode of the water look is the tone "klar", with light text on clear water.
5. **Everything is dynamically animated** (alive at rest, answering touch and scrolling), but calm. Off with reduced motion.
6. **Template first.** The look is prepared as two templates to look at (mobile, desktop) with a guide; the app itself is not changed until he says so.
7. **Life at rest: the strength stays 1, the pace becomes a little faster (14.11., 10:50).** Shown side by side, strength 1 and 1.5 cannot be told apart by eye; the lever is the pace of the wandering lights. His words: "beim Tempo bin ich mir nicht so sicher, aber ich denke schon ein bisschen schneller." So: a LITTLE faster than built (24 / 34 / 40 / 46 s a lap), clearly less than the 2.5-fold trial he was shown. To be built and shown to him before it goes live.
8. **The light mode ("klar") a little lighter (14.11., 10:50).** About the desktop panels: "bei dem hellen noch ein bisschen heller. Vielleicht auch sogar bei der mobilen Version noch ein bisschen heller." So: tone "klar" a little lighter on the desktop, and probably on the phone too - within the contrast floors (titles 7, text 4.5, placeholders 3). "dunkel" stays as it is unless he says otherwise. To be built and shown to him. Made precise at 11:00: "Also mit hellem meine ich durchsichtiger. Aber bitte vorerst nur beim hellen Modus." So lighter means MORE SEE-THROUGH (more of the scene through the water), not a lighter colour - and only the tone "klar" for now.
9. **The phone version may go live (14.11., 10:50).** "Die Handy-Version können wir gerne schon auf die echte Seite laden." The mobile integration (branch welt/wasser-mobil, commit c7e8299) was put on the staging site first, checked there, then merged into main and delivered to the live site by a deploy helper chat. The desktop stays unchanged until he has seen the desktop integration on his own screen.
10. **Soft leaving (14.11., 11:00).** Asked whether bottom sheets and drawers should close softly and ticked or removed rows should leave softly, he said: "Punkt 6 hätte ich schon gerne, ja." This needs small changes in the app itself (the sheet host, lists, the toast) and is built after the integrations, for phone and desktop.
11. **First run on a real phone (14.11., 11:05, staging site, the mobile integration c7e8299).** His words: "am Handy läuft bei der Testseite eigentlich alles sehr schön flüssig. Das Einzige, was mir jetzt noch ein bisschen aufgefallen wäre, wenn ich unten auf den Reiter klicke, dauert es kurz ein paar Millisekunden und dann wechselt er. Aber dann flüssig." So the frame rate that was feared from the software WebKit build is fine on his phone. Open from this test: the short wait between the tap on a tab and the change of screen - to be looked at (what happens at a change of tab: new scene image for the drops, helpers for every card, the marker) and shortened, or at least answered at once under the finger.
12. **The day card, on the phone and on the desktop (14.11., 11:22).** Shown the phone Start with a day card ("Tageskarte": greeting, clock, date, weather, daylight track, season, solar window and quote in one card instead of loose lines over the scene) and asked whether (1) it should go live on the phone and (2) the desktop Start should get the same, he said: "Ja, bitte 1 und 2 umsetzen. Und die Tageskarte auch am Desktop, ja." Phone: live since 14.11., 11:30 (main 65b1e44). Desktop: built on branch welt/tageskarte-desktop (9c4afa7, shared component src/ui/Tageskarte.tsx), merged into wasser-look.
13. **The desktop may go live; the fine-tuning follows in the evening (14.11., 13:05).** The desktop with the look and the day card was on the staging site from 12:08. Offered two ways (deliver the staging state now and the fine-tuning in the evening as a second delivery, or everything in the evening), he said: "Okay, dann mach das bitte so und liefere dann am Abend das Update nach." So: main was moved to 9c4afa7 and a deploy helper chat delivers it to the live site; the fine-tuning (pace, tone "klar" more see-through, soft leaving, tab change) goes to the staging site first and then live this evening, with before/after pictures for him. Way back for the live site: 65b1e44. Not verified before this delivery: his own graphics card, Safari, sign-in and sync with this build (the server part is unchanged).
14. **First run of the desktop look on a real screen (14.11., 15:15, live site, state 9c4afa7).** His words: "bei mir am PC läuft alles wunderbar gut, aber ich weiß natürlich nicht, wie es bei einem schlechteren PC ist, da ich eine RTX 4090 habe." So: smooth on a very strong graphics card; weaker PCs (integrated graphics, older laptops) are untested. Known from this work machine (no GPU, software rendering - worse than any real PC): the blur of the sheets costs 15 to 29 % of the frames; the drops have a governor that lowers their quality when frames get slow, the blur has none. To do: measure the desktop as a worst case on the work machine after the fine-tuning (with and without the look, governor level, frame times), and if it is too heavy, an automatic saving mode that also takes the blur down.
